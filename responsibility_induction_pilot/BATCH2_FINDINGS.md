# Responsibility Induction Pilot — Batch 2 Findings

## Batch

Batch 2 contains eight verbatim hypothetical desired behaviors from Practical
Trigger-Action Programming and four device-only SmartSense Routine sequences.
The former preserves participant wording but is not deployed or longitudinal;
the latter is behaviorally sourced but lacks trigger, action direction, user,
household, beneficiary, and purpose.

Both coders independently judged all eight participant statements as only
`compatible_only` support for a possible standing responsibility. Both judged
all four device-only SmartSense sequences `irrelevant` to responsibility
semantics without linked purpose evidence. They also agreed that all twelve
units lacked an explicit failure meaning.

## What the disagreement means

The poor task/preference/goal agreement revealed a category-design error. A
statement such as "I would like the floor cleaned daily while nobody is in the
room" is simultaneously:

- a preference in speech-act form;
- a requested behavior;
- an event/recurrence-conditioned task;
- a possible implementation of a broader cleanliness responsibility.

These are orthogonal properties, not mutually exclusive classes. Forcing one
label creates arbitrary disagreement without improving the responsibility gate.
V3 therefore encodes semantic content, temporal scope, ownership, purpose
uniqueness, and slot completeness separately.

## Candidate groupings

The eight participant statements generate useful hypotheses but no admitted
responsibility:

1. **Adaptive indoor conditions:** fan-on-heat, daylight-responsive dimming,
   and bright-sun blind closure suggest thermal/visual comfort mechanisms. They
   come from one study with unavailable respondent IDs and do not state failure,
   release, or persistent ownership.
2. **Event-relative preparation and awareness:** coffee before an alarm,
   package notification during absence, and pet-boundary notification encode
   anticipation or awareness, but their beneficiaries, harms, and broader
   purposes differ and should not be merged merely because they reference
   events.
3. **Occupancy-aware household activity:** daily unoccupied-room cleaning and
   night movement lighting include recurrence/context, but their purposes and
   risks are not explicit.

The SmartSense sequences cannot join any semantic cluster. They may later verify
that a frozen responsibility has observed implementation mechanisms, but they
cannot name or validate that responsibility.

## Decision

No new responsibility is admitted. The evidence pool now contains 24 units and
five audited source cards, but the strongest gap is no longer rule quantity. It
is linked, independent-household evidence containing purpose, ongoing ownership,
failure meaning, and override/release. Future collection should target those
fields rather than add more unlabeled device sequences.
